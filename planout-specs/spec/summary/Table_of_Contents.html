1.  components
1.1Overview

The Table of Contents (TOC) feature provides a persistent navigation panel on the left side of the document viewer. It acts as a roadmap for the document, allowing users to quickly identify their current location and jump to specific sections. By organizing the document structure clearly and providing direct links to content, this feature improves readability and makes navigating long, complex documents much more efficient.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Protocol Identification: At the top of the navigation panel, the system displays the unique Protocol ID in a distinct, easy-to-read badge format.
2.Section Listing: The system generates a list of all document sections.
3.Hierarchical Organization: The TOC automatically detects sub-sections (based on their numbering format) and indents them under their parent sections to create a clear visual hierarchy.
4.Active Highlighting: As the user scrolls through the document, the TOC automatically highlights the section currently being viewed, ensuring the user always knows their place.
5.Interactive Navigation: Users can click any item in the TOC to instantly scroll the main document view to that specific section.
6.Document Summary: The footer of the navigation panel provides a quick summary, including the current version of the document and the total number of sections available.
1.3Key Concepts
–Protocol ID: A unique identifier for the document, displayed prominently to ensure users are working on the correct file.
–Hierarchical Indentation: A visual formatting style where sub-sections are slightly offset to the right, making it easier to distinguish between main topics and supporting details.
–Active State: A visual indicator (highlighting) that links the user's current scroll position in the document to the corresponding entry in the navigation menu.
–Smooth Scrolling: An interactive behavior where clicking a menu item triggers a fluid transition to the selected content, rather than an abrupt jump.
1.4Business Value
–Improved User Efficiency: By providing a "one-click" navigation system, users spend less time scrolling through long documents to find specific information.
–Enhanced Clarity: The structured, indented layout helps users understand the document's organization at a glance, reducing cognitive load.
–Context Awareness: The active highlighting and version tracking in the footer ensure that users remain oriented and confident that they are referencing the correct version of the document.
2.  LOGIC
2.1Overview

The Table of Contents feature provides a structured way to navigate and manage document content. It acts as a dynamic sidebar that lists all sections of a document, allowing users to easily add new content areas and jump directly to specific parts of their document.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initial Loading: When you open a document, the system automatically retrieves the full list of existing sections, including their titles, content, and organizational order.
2.Adding a Section: When you click the "Add Section" button, the system creates a new section entry. Once created, the interface automatically selects this new section and scrolls your view directly to it so you can begin editing immediately.
3.Adding a Subsection: Similarly, when you click the "Add Subsection" button, the system creates a new subsection entry. The interface then automatically selects and scrolls to this new subsection, keeping your workflow seamless.
2.3Key Concepts
–Section: A distinct block of content within your document that can be managed, titled, and ordered independently.
–Auto-Selection & Scrolling: A user-experience feature that ensures that whenever you create a new part of a document, the screen immediately focuses on that new area so you don't have to search for it.
–AI Labeling: A status indicator attached to sections that identifies whether the content was generated by AI or authored by a human.
2.4Business Value

This feature significantly improves document readability and authoring efficiency. By providing a clear Table of Contents, users can maintain a high-level view of long documents, navigate complex information quickly, and add new sections without losing their place. This reduces the time spent manually scrolling and organizing content, allowing users to focus on the quality of the document itself.

3.  DATA
3.1Overview

The Table of Contents feature provides a structured, navigable sidebar for your documents. It automatically organizes the document into distinct sections, allowing users to see the document's outline at a glance and jump directly to specific parts of the content. This feature ensures that as you build or edit a document, the navigation panel stays perfectly in sync with the document's structure.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Retrieval: When you open a document, the system fetches all associated sections, automatically arranging them in the correct order based on their position in the document.
2.Display: These sections are listed in the left-hand panel, creating a clear Table of Contents for easy navigation.
3.Creation: When you add a new section or subsection, the system automatically assigns it a default status, version, and position at the end of the document.
4.Synchronization: Whenever a new section is added, the system updates the document’s "last modified" timestamp, ensuring that your document history remains accurate and up-to-date.
3.3Key Concepts
–Section: A distinct, titled part of a document that acts as an entry in the Table of Contents.
–Positioning: The system automatically tracks the order of sections, ensuring the Table of Contents always reflects the logical flow of the document.
–Document Metadata: Background information, such as the last modified date, which is automatically updated whenever the document structure changes.
–Draft Status: New sections are created as "drafts" by default, allowing you to build out your document structure before finalizing the content.
3.4Business Value
–Improved Navigation: Users can quickly jump to relevant information without scrolling through long documents, significantly reducing time spent searching.
–Enhanced Organization: By enforcing a structured approach to sections, the feature helps maintain consistency across all documents in the system.
–Increased Efficiency: Automated section management—such as auto-positioning and versioning—removes manual administrative tasks, allowing users to focus on the document content itself.
