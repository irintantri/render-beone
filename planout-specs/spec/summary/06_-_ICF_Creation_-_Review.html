1.  Frontend
1.1Overview

The ICF Review feature provides a dedicated workspace for stakeholders to examine, discuss, and refine draft Informed Consent Forms (ICFs). This interface allows users to view the document structure, track changes, and collaborate through comments, ensuring that all feedback is captured and addressed before the document is finalized.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Review Actions: Access the edit bar to compare different versions of the document, allowing you to see how the content has evolved.
2.Comment Actions: Use the dedicated comments panel on the right to view feedback from reviewers or add your own notes directly to the document.
3.Progress Stepper: Monitor your current stage in the document creation process via the visual progress indicator.
4.Audit Trail: Review the chronological history of the document, including all edits, comments, and AI-generated updates, clearly marked by the person or system that performed the action.
5.Global Navigation: Use the top header to manage your profile and navigate the platform.
6.Bottom Bar: Utilize the persistent footer to perform final review actions and move the document forward.
7.Wrapper Shell: View essential document metadata, such as the protocol name and ID, displayed in the main layout.
8.Table of Contents: Use the left-hand navigation panel to jump quickly to specific sections of the document.
9.Document Section Cards: Read the document content, which is organized into clear, individual cards. Each card identifies whether the content was AI-generated, ensuring transparency in how the draft was created.
1.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A complete, time-stamped record of all activity on the document, helping teams maintain accountability and transparency.
–Section Cards: A modular way of displaying document content that makes long forms easier to read and review.
–AI-Generated Badge: A visual indicator that highlights content drafted by the system, allowing reviewers to focus their attention on verifying AI-assisted sections.
–Version Comparison: The ability to view changes between different drafts to ensure that previous feedback has been correctly incorporated.
1.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the document approval process by centralizing communication and history. By providing a clear view of AI-generated content alongside human feedback, it reduces the time spent on manual reconciliation, minimizes errors in the consent process, and ensures that all stakeholders are aligned on the final version of the document.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The ICF Review feature provides a transparent and organized way for users to manage the document approval process. It allows reviewers and authors to see the current state of an Informed Consent Form (ICF) and access a complete, chronological history of all actions taken on that document. This ensures that feedback, status changes, and document updates are tracked clearly, making it easy to incorporate reviewer comments and maintain accountability throughout the drafting process.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Retrieval: The system loads the specific ICF draft, displaying its current status, metadata, and content so the user can review the latest version.
2.Audit Trail Access: The system retrieves the full history of the document, including every action taken by users.
3.Chronological Display: The system organizes these actions from newest to oldest, ensuring the most recent feedback or status updates appear at the top.
4.Review and Action: The user reviews the document content alongside the audit history to understand previous feedback and incorporate necessary changes.
2.3Key Concepts
–Audit Trail: A comprehensive, time-stamped record of all activities related to a document, such as who viewed it, who provided feedback, and when the status changed.
–Document Metadata: Essential information about the ICF, including its name, current version, and last modified date, which helps users identify the correct draft.
–Chronological Feed: A list of events sorted by time, allowing users to follow the "story" of the document's development from start to finish.
2.4Business Value
–Accountability: By maintaining a tamper-evident history of all actions, the organization can ensure full traceability for compliance and regulatory requirements.
–Efficiency: Reviewers and authors can quickly understand the document's history and see exactly what feedback has been provided, reducing the time spent searching for context.
–Transparency: Providing a clear view of status transitions and user comments minimizes confusion during the review cycle, leading to faster and more accurate document finalization.
3.  Data
3.1Overview

The ICF Review feature allows you to access and evaluate the current draft of an Informed Consent Form (ICF). This tool provides a clear view of the document's status and a detailed history of all changes and feedback provided by reviewers. By centralizing the document content and its activity log, the system ensures that you can easily track the evolution of the document and incorporate necessary feedback to finalize the draft.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Document Retrieval: The system pulls the specific ICF draft you wish to review, ensuring you are looking at the most current version of the document content and its associated metadata.
2.Activity Review: The system retrieves the audit trail for that specific document.
3.Chronological Feed: The audit logs are displayed in a timeline format, starting with the most recent actions and comments, allowing you to see exactly what feedback was provided and by whom.
4.Feedback Incorporation: Using the document content and the insights from the audit trail, you can address reviewer comments and update the document as needed.
3.3Key Concepts
–ICF Document: The core file containing the consent information, including its current version, status, and the actual text that requires review.
–Audit Trail: A chronological record of all actions taken on the document, which serves as the primary source for tracking reviewer feedback and history.
–Metadata: Background information attached to the document that helps identify its version, creation date, and last modification time.
–Chronological Timeline: A view that organizes activity logs from newest to oldest, ensuring the most recent feedback is always at the top for immediate attention.
3.4Business Value

This feature streamlines the document approval process by eliminating the need to hunt for feedback across different channels. By providing a transparent, time-stamped history of all interactions, it reduces the risk of missing critical reviewer comments. This leads to faster turnaround times for ICF drafts, improved accuracy in documentation, and a clear, accountable record of the entire review lifecycle.
