1.  COMPONENTS
1.1Overview

The AI Document Editor is a tool designed to help you refine and polish document content directly within the "Edit and Refine" page. It acts as an intelligent writing assistant, allowing you to select specific sections of a document and provide instructions—such as simplifying language or summarizing key points—to automatically update the text.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Select Section: You begin by viewing the document section you wish to edit. The content is displayed in an editable text box, allowing you to make manual changes or review the text before applying AI improvements.
2.Choose a Quick Prompt: To save time, you can select from pre-defined buttons (such as "Simplify language for patient readability") which automatically populate your instruction.
3.Add Custom Instructions: If you have a specific request, you can type your own instructions into the text box provided.
4.Ask AI: Once your instruction is ready, click the "Ask AI" button. The system will process your request, update the document section with the AI-generated improvements, and provide visual feedback while it works.
1.3Key Concepts
–Section Context: The specific portion of the document the AI is currently focused on, ensuring that edits are relevant and targeted.
–Quick Prompts: Shortcut buttons that provide common editing commands, helping you achieve consistent results without needing to type out instructions.
–Instruction Textarea: A flexible input field where you can provide natural-language guidance to the AI, such as asking for a tone change or a compliance check.
–Processing State: A visual indicator that lets you know the AI is currently working on your request, preventing duplicate actions while the document is being updated.
1.4Business Value

This feature significantly reduces the time and effort required to draft and refine professional documents. By providing both quick-access shortcuts and the ability to give custom instructions, it empowers users to maintain high-quality, readable, and compliant content with minimal manual effort. It bridges the gap between complex technical documentation and clear, patient-friendly communication.

2.  Logic
2.1Overview

The AI Document Editor feature allows users to refine specific sections of a document using natural language instructions. By selecting a section and providing a prompt (such as "make this more concise" or "adjust the tone to be more professional"), the system leverages AI to rewrite the content while maintaining the document's structure and tracking the history of the changes.

2.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Instruction Selection: The user selects a specific section of the document and provides a written instruction (prompt) describing how they would like that section modified.
2.Content Processing: The system combines the existing section text with the user's instructions and sends them to the AI engine.
3.AI Revision: The AI processes the request and generates a revised version of the section based on the provided instructions.
4.System Update: The system automatically updates the document with the new content, marks the section as "AI Edited," updates its status to "AI Generated," and increments the version number to ensure a clear audit trail.
2.3Key Concepts
–Prompt: The natural language instruction provided by the user to guide the AI on how to rewrite or refine the selected text.
–Section Context: The specific portion of the document being targeted for editing, which serves as the foundation for the AI's work.
–Provenance Tag: A label (e.g., "AI Edited") attached to the section that identifies the origin of the content, ensuring transparency in how the document was created.
–Version Control: An automated tracking mechanism that increments the version number each time an AI edit is applied, allowing users to keep track of changes over time.
2.4Business Value

This feature significantly reduces the time and effort required for document drafting and refinement. By allowing users to apply targeted AI edits to specific sections, the tool ensures high-quality, consistent documentation while maintaining full control over the final output. The automated status and version tracking provide peace of mind by ensuring that all AI-assisted changes are clearly documented and easily manageable within the broader workflow.

3.  Data
3.1Overview

The AI Document Editor feature provides a structured way to manage and track changes made to document sections by artificial intelligence. It ensures that every piece of content generated or refined by the AI is properly labeled, tracked for version history, and assigned a specific status within the document workflow. This allows users to maintain full oversight of AI-assisted content while ensuring the document remains organized and accurate.

3.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Data Receipt: The system receives a structured update containing the revised content for a specific section of a document.
2.Validation: The system checks the incoming data to ensure all required information is present, such as the section title, the current version number, and the specific status of the content.
3.Constraint Checking: The system verifies that the data meets business rules, such as ensuring the section position is valid and the version number is current.
4.Categorization: The system assigns a "provenance tag" to the content, identifying whether it was generated, edited, or revised by the AI, or if it was manually created.
5.Persistence: Once validated, the updated section is saved to the document, keeping the document’s structure and history intact.
3.3Key Concepts
–Provenance Tag: A label that clearly identifies the origin of the content (e.g., whether it was created by AI or a human), providing transparency into how the text was produced.
–Workflow Status: A tracking mechanism that indicates the current state of a section, such as whether it is a "Draft," "Needs Review," or "Approved."
–Version Control: A counter that tracks the number of revisions made to a section, ensuring that users can always identify the most recent update.
–Section Positioning: A numerical index that determines the order of the content within the document, ensuring that sections appear in the correct sequence.
3.4Business Value

This feature brings transparency and reliability to AI-assisted document creation. By clearly labeling AI contributions and tracking the status of every section, teams can confidently use AI to draft or refine documents while maintaining a clear audit trail. This reduces the risk of errors, simplifies the review process, and ensures that all document changes are accounted for and properly managed within the existing workflow.
