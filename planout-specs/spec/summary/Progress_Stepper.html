1.  Components
1.1Overview

The Progress Stepper is a visual navigation tool displayed at the top of the creation screens in the BeOne platform. It provides users with a clear sense of where they are in the process and how much work remains. The stepper guides users through five distinct stages: Setup, Generate, Edit & Refine, Review, and Preview.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Visual Tracking: As you move through the creation process, the stepper automatically updates to show your current location, which steps you have already finished, and which steps are still ahead.
2.Status Indicators: Each step is visually represented based on its status:

* Done: Steps you have completed are marked with a check icon.

* Active: The step you are currently working on is highlighted to stand out.

* Future: Steps you have not yet reached are grayed out.

3.Navigation: You can click on previously completed steps to return to them, provided the system has enough information (such as a saved document ID) to load that specific page.
4.System Constraints: The "Generate" step is a temporary, automated process and cannot be clicked to navigate back to. Additionally, later stages like "Edit & Refine," "Review," and "Preview" require a saved document to be accessible.
1.3Key Concepts
–Active Step: The specific stage of the process currently displayed on your screen.
–Completed Steps: Stages that have been finished, allowing you to revisit them if needed.
–Future Steps: Stages that are locked until you progress further through the workflow.
–Transient Step: A step (like "Generate") that acts as a bridge between stages and does not serve as a destination you can navigate to manually.
–Document ID: A unique identifier required to ensure that when you navigate back to a previous step, the system loads the correct work-in-progress.
1.4Business Value

The Progress Stepper improves the user experience by providing transparency and predictability. By clearly showing the path from start to finish, it reduces user confusion, helps manage expectations regarding the length of the process, and allows for easy navigation back to previous steps to make adjustments without losing context.
