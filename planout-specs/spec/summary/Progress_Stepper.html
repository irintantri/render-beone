1.  Components
1.1Overview

The Progress Stepper is a visual navigation tool located at the top of the creation screens in the BeOne platform. It provides users with a clear roadmap of the five-stage creation process, helping them understand their current progress and how much work remains. By displaying the journey from initial setup to final preview, the stepper ensures a consistent and intuitive experience as users move through the application.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Initialization: The stepper displays five distinct stages: Setup, Generate, Edit & Refine, Review, and Preview.
2.Visual Status Update: As you move through the process, the stepper automatically updates the appearance of each stage:

* Done: Previous steps are marked with a checkmark to show they are complete.

* Active: The current step is highlighted to indicate where you are.

* Future: Upcoming steps are grayed out to show they are not yet reached.

3.Navigation Control: You can click on previous steps to return to them, provided the system has enough information (such as a saved document) to allow you to go back.
4.Transient Handling: The "Generate" step is a temporary process stage and cannot be clicked to navigate back to, ensuring the system maintains a stable workflow.
1.3Key Concepts
–Progress Stepper: A horizontal navigation bar that tracks your location within a multi-step task.
–Active Step: The current screen you are viewing, which is visually highlighted to provide context.
–Transient Step: A specific stage in the process (the "Generate" phase) that is automated or temporary and does not support manual navigation.
–Document Dependency: A requirement where certain later stages (Edit, Review, Preview) are only accessible once a document has been successfully created and assigned an ID.
1.4Business Value
–Improved User Experience: Reduces user frustration by providing a clear sense of direction and progress, preventing users from feeling "lost" in complex workflows.
–Reduced Training Needs: The intuitive design acts as a self-guiding interface, allowing new users to understand the platform's requirements without extensive documentation.
–Workflow Integrity: By controlling which steps are clickable based on the status of the document, the system prevents users from accessing stages before the necessary data is ready, ensuring data consistency and reducing errors.
