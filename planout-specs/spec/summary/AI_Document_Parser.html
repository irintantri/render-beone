1.  LOGIC
1.1Overview

The AI Document Parser is an automated system designed to streamline the creation of Informed Consent Forms (ICFs). By combining a standardized document template with specific clinical protocol details, the system uses artificial intelligence to extract relevant information and populate the form automatically. This reduces manual drafting time while ensuring that complex clinical information is translated into clear, participant-friendly language.

1.2Step-by-Step Flow
1.Template Selection: The system retrieves the requested ICF template and breaks it down into distinct sections and specific placeholders that need to be filled.
2.Protocol Retrieval: The system locates the corresponding clinical protocol document (PDF) required for the specific study.
3.Text Extraction: The system converts the PDF into raw text so the information can be processed.
4.Skeleton Mapping: The system organizes the template’s placeholders, mapping each one to the specific section of the document where it belongs. This provides the AI with the necessary context to understand what information is needed for each part of the form.
5.AI Processing: The system sends the protocol text and the template requirements to an AI model. The AI extracts the relevant data and formats it into a structured list. If the AI’s initial response is formatted incorrectly, the system automatically retries once to ensure accuracy.
6.Form Population: The system inserts the extracted information into the template. If any information is missing from the source document, the system inserts a clear placeholder note so a human reviewer knows exactly what needs to be completed.
7.Artifact Storage: The final document, the underlying data structure, and the extraction results are saved to the system for review and future use.
8.Unique Identification: The system assigns a unique, standardized ID to the newly generated document for tracking and record-keeping purposes.
1.3Key Concepts
–ICF (Informed Consent Form): The document provided to study participants that explains the risks, benefits, and procedures of a clinical trial.
–Template: A pre-defined document structure containing placeholders that act as "fill-in-the-blank" fields.
–Protocol: The official document detailing the clinical study's design, objectives, and procedures.
–Placeholder: A specific tag in the template (e.g., {{Study_Duration}}) that the AI identifies and replaces with actual data from the protocol.
–Artifacts: The final files generated by the process, including the completed document and the supporting data used to create it.
1.4Business Value
–Efficiency: Significantly reduces the time required to draft complex consent forms by automating the extraction of data from lengthy clinical protocols.
–Consistency: Ensures that all ICFs follow a standardized format and tone, reducing the risk of human error or omission.
–Clarity: The AI is instructed to use plain, natural language, which improves participant comprehension and ensures the consent process is more accessible.
–Auditability: By saving the extraction results and the document structure alongside the final form, the system provides a clear trail of how the information was derived, supporting compliance and quality assurance.
